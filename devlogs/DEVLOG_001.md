### 🌟 1. The Birth of Chud Studios
Welcome to the very first devlog from **Chud Studios**! 

We started with a wild question: *What if the fast-paced, synergy-stacked combat of **Hades** collided at full throttle with a runaway steam locomotive?*

Today, we are officially christening our studio and unveiling our flagship game in active development: **Next Stop (2026): Locomotive Oblivion**. We build punchy, reactive roguelite action games with zero filler, crunchy screen-shake, and relentless momentum.

---

### 🧢 2. Meet Our Mascot: "Lil' Chud"!
Every great game studio needs a figurehead. Meet **Lil' Chud**:

Lil' Chud is a wacky, energetic cartoon locomotive who sports a crooked conductor cap, clutches an arcade joystick and a monkey wrench, and puffs circular smoke rings. 

In our brand-new animated opening splash screen, Lil' Chud drops out of the sky with rubbery cartoon squash-and-stretch physics (`*BOING!*`), stares right at your mouse cursor with interactive googly eyes, and gives a cheerful whistle (`*TOOT TOOT!*`) before sending you into Grand Central Station.

### 💥 3. Where the Game Stands Right Now
For our alpha foundations, we engineered high-octane game systems built on **Python 3.13 & Pygame-CE**:

- ⚔️ **4 Distinct Starter Weapons**:
  - *Pneumatic Riveter*: Pinpoint high-rate-of-fire nail gun.
  - *Stoker's Heavy Cleaver*: 180-degree sweeping melee arc that sends enemies flying and repels bullets.
  - *Coal Scattergun*: 5-pellet blunderbuss with brutal point-blank criticals.
  - *Tesla Arc Caster*: Piercing electric plasma spheres that chain between swarms.
- ⚡ **9 Stackable Boon Synergies (Tier 1 ➔ Tier 3 Evolution)**:
  - Chain lightning, Molten Fuel fire puddles, Cryo Condensers that freeze enemies solid, Ricochet rounds, and Life-steal feasts.
- 🛡️ **Defensive Blocking & Super Abilities**:
  - Timed parries with `[SHIFT]` deflect incoming attacks.
  - High-voltage super abilities (`Overdrive`, `Tesla Rail`, `Cataclysm`) charged with `[E]`.
- 🚊 **Dynamic Train Cars**:
  - Narrow coach aisles, breakable dining tables, slippery cold-storage freezer cars, and explosive fuel barrels.
- 👹 **The Conductor Boss & Elite Factions**:
  - Distinct bipedal enemy castes (Steam, Derelict, Subway, Cryo, Infernal) and the multi-phase Conductor Boss with wrench salvos, steam shockwaves, and an overclocked furnace rush!
- 📖 **The Conductor's Enemy Archives (Codex)**:
  - Complete bestiary in Grand Central Hub detailing lore, behavior, and attack telegraphs for all 10 locomotive adversaries.
- 🛠️ **In-Game Test Mode & Dev Console**:
  - Full dev console accessible via `[F1]` to freely warp to any car, level, or boss, tune stats, and test builds.

### 🌐 4. What's Next: WebAssembly Browser Playtests!
No one should have to download giant installers just to test an alpha demo. 

We have deployed our **WebAssembly (Pygbag) build pipeline** powered by GitHub Actions. Soon, **Next Stop** will be playable instantly in any modern web browser on desktop and mobile via **GitHub Pages** and **itch.io**.
